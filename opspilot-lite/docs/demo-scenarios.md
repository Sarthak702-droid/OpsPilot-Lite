# Demo scenarios

`make seed` creates 100 products, 30 suppliers, 200 customers, 2,000 sales, 500 invoices, 400 payments, and 1,500 inventory transactions in a dedicated demo organization. Tile X21 has 80 units, 20 sales per day, and a 6 day lead time, producing a HIGH stockout signal and a Go calculated order quantity. ABC Traders has a ₹120,000 invoice 47 days overdue. XYZ Metals has five delivered purchase orders, three late, producing 40% on-time performance and a HIGH supplier signal. The user facing UI needs a real Clerk organization and imported data; the demo organization itself is not a login account.
